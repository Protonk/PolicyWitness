# Worker report: skeptical assessment

Material exposure: I read worker-first.md, the complete worker interaction record, the onramp and actor example, and all 27 pages of WORKER.pdf through a direct layout-text derivative. I also inspected supplied page images 1, 7 and 16 for the figures and table layout. An initially truncated extraction was replaced by complete page batches. No linked targets, repository implementation, tests, other reports or external material were inspected; nothing was executed beyond document reading. This is an assessment of the report against its artifact and history, not independent certification of implementation or cited checks.

The principal work result is supported. Page 1 explicitly separates prepared report storage from attempt-time target acquisition, and pp. 4–6 explain fresh acquisitions, retained observations and changes that alter the experiment. Pages 2 and 4–5 distinguish token-buffer erasure, native release, later access and publication. The example contains no post-release read. The recommendation therefore follows without assuming successful execution, particular native release effects or additional documentation. The report also accurately preserves the consequential incomplete-cleanup distinction: declaration host erasure is independent, whereas actor shared teardown requires a reaped or never-launched owner and private custody has separate ownership (pp. 2, 4–5).

The favorable clarity judgment needs bounded interpretation. The reader had already rejected pre-opening and inferred no revocation from erasure at checkpoint 2, before receiving the PDF, in direct response to the supplied problematic sketch. Successful repetition of those conclusions is weak evidence that the document alone teaches them to an unprompted reader. Stronger evidence is the subsequent accurate account of PW-specific mappings, publication, separate actor custody and cleanup gates. Also, checkpoint 3 records opening exposure through early p. 3 and the p. 1 image, not the requested complete-artifact heading/figure skim. Its first impression cannot establish immediate discoverability across the entire PDF. These are limits on the reading evidence, not demonstrated defects in the document.

The report slightly misdates its correction. Checkpoint 3 actually says reporting resources are prepared beforehand; checkpoint 5, before targeted questions, explicitly recognizes attempt-time exec reporting resources. Checkpoint 6 then compares the exact opening and confirms the correction. The PDF's opening already names exec resources inside attempts; pp. 4, 6 and the p. 7 cluster reinforce this. There was real initial overcompression, subsequently corrected by fuller reading and then examined under prompting. There is no supported contradiction and no demonstrated consequence for the file-operation recommendation.

The actor-coverage judgment is reasonable for this review. Page 1 labels the conceptual map as declaration-form; p. 6 similarly limits the detailed authority-channel inventory. The crosswalk and p. 5 supply the actor relationships needed to reason about ownership and incomplete cleanup. They do not constitute an implementation specification for the entire actor protocol, and the report appropriately reserves exact admission, ordering and decoding rules to linked material. A full actor diagram is not established as necessary work by this record.

The navigation complaint is credible, but does not establish a missing central explanation. The p. 7 image is a very tall, widely connected inventory; p. 16 visually confirms narrow columns, wrapped identifiers and extensive repeated assertion text. Those impose actual PDF lookup costs. The source-level choice is a detailed ledger, while conversion expands the reference material into a continuous sequence; the report correctly avoids treating PDF length as proof of the source's reading burden. For the assigned resource review, the relevant distinctions are already narrated on pp. 1–6.

No overlooked issue overturns the work result. The report's hypothetical risks concerning universal isolation, actor/declaration conflation and erasure-as-revocation should not be promoted into observed misunderstandings. Page 3 bounds launch observation L particularly strongly, including unavailable original raw evidence and lack of established current-source applicability; pp. 6, 8 and 26–27 distinguish citation mechanics from semantic support. These qualifications support the report's refusal to certify facts from citation presence. Repeating them more often, duplicating the complete actor transport graph, or changing the precise opening to repair the reader's broader paraphrase is not warranted by this reading evidence.
