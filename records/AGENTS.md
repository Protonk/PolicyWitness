# records/

This directory contains prose records of user or development problems:
observations, reproduction steps, investigations, and their evidence. These
records provide background for associated plans; they are not active plans or
the repository's current behavior contract.

Repository inbound links to a record must come only from its associated
`*-PLAN.md` files. Do not link records from permanent documentation, source,
tests, indexes, or unrelated plans. Associated plans carry the connection
between an investigation and proposed work.

Records may link outward to relevant source, documentation, and evidence.
Keep those links valid when moving a record. State when linked evidence is
gitignored and available only in the local checkout.
