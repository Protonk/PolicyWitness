# Independent trigger review — worker language

Frozen revision: `2d63c983224907b109b4349d216c03471a8b1f0c`. First inventory remains immutable. All 323 first-inventory IDs and six separate follow-up IDs were reviewed. This is descriptive classification, not a truth audit or an editing recommendation.

The principal disagreement is the use of `Sandbox-gated` as an affirmative trigger. It names an inquiry. It does not say that a resource was measured, a cited test was run, or private state was observed. The caption/check organization does invite assertion-coverage overreading; that is the narrower inference covered by N057/N305/N306. It should not automatically turn every source-only or unknown cell into C2.

The JSON records every decision, the frozen source quote, original and suggested classifications, original and replacement triggers, confidence changes, and the precise mistaken inference when identifiable. Some C2 confirmations replace an unrelated trigger rather than changing the bucket. Some B1 units are mixed; partial-trigger flags prevent one supported inference from standing in for an entire compound guard.

## Main decisions

- Exact local triggers replace generic freshness for normal-launch generalization (N012), signal controls (N014), and the low-descriptor arrangement (N019). Freshness does not establish a complete census; N020/N054/N307/N309 become D2.
- Positive examined-source allocation/formatting claims replace generic static-storage triggers in N026/N232/N234/N281/N283. The immediate existing-storage/acquisition account supports N043. No allocation or locale behavior was tested here.
- N040 remains B1/C2 with medium confidence and explicit partial support. Release/read sequence supports revocation/permission overreading; the state-carrying handle supports kernel-state overreading. Stable identity and private allocation cannot inherit those triggers without their own analysis.
- N028’s child setup/observation text does not invite its exact nonzero-exit-negates-spawn inference; N028 becomes D2. Likewise, source-described Mach-right lifetime does not assert a live right count (N032).
- N300 changes D2 to C2 at medium confidence: WORKER explicitly joins denial-log candidates to run evidence (lines 153 and 214), a specific local invitation to causal attribution. All other original D2 units remain D2 conditionally, or are excluded for issuer-only scope.
- Actual failed-setup spawning, demonstrated inheritance, nil-environment corroboration and outside-worker provenance cells must not become guards merely by column placement. N074/N076/N126/N135/N251/N258 and N285/N287/N289/N291/N293 receive A1 proposals, with mixed-role flags where needed.

## Scope boundary

Each excluded JSON decision retains its original bucket/split only for traceability and explicitly sets `excluded: true`; those fields are not a final taxonomy assignment.

Read as free-standing issuer-no-child statements, they would be excluded. Host-side facts about actual worker input/reporting objects, delivered authority or lifetime/transfer boundaries remain A1. F001/F003/F004/F005 are retained; F002/F006 are issuer-only exclusions.
