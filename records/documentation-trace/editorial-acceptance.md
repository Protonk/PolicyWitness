# Editorial acceptance of the developer reading paths

Read-only review on 2026-11-15 for the
[documentation trace plan](../../DOCUMENTATION-TRACE-PLAN.md), after routing,
the `part` retirement, protocol consolidation and the actor-advancement
correction. This review reads the final documents against the interface/example,
controlled-read and protocol investigations. It executes no generator, test,
mutation, build or native workload. Mechanical preservation and final-source
execution evidence remain the integrating executor's separate work.

The three reading paths are usable. Within the bounded traces and protocol
readings below, no remaining public-meaning conflict or need for another
ARCHITECTURE promotion was found. The long references and partial associations
are visible limitations, not claims of exhaustive change detection.

## What request can I supply, and what obligations accompany it?

[REQUEST](../../docs/REQUEST.md) starts with declaration, controller preparation,
host admission and contingent execution. Its publication table separates
executable help facts, the example's build admission promise and the controlled
input's positional publication without a general admission promise. The
failed-publication rule precedes the examples. The observable-create sequence
then introduces one-change refusals, phases, extension operations, actors and
the controller/direct-XPC distinction before the field reference.

The reader can follow `--runner-mode` from the entry-point discussion to
[selector precedence](../../docs/REQUEST.md#contract-request-options-precedence).
Malformed shadowed aliases, an explicitly empty nested entitlement list and
CLI processing before request-file reading retain their meanings. The account
explicitly says the interface association does not compare the spelling or
free-text precedence mechanically. Test overrides have their own declaration
section and an echoed value is not credited as an observed branch effect.

The difficult transition is from a request admitted at one boundary to work
admitted at another. The request corpus, exact failure paths and the explicit
publication/host distinction make that transition usable. The complete
reference remains long; it is reached by subject links rather than requiring
the reader to start with field entries. User-guide links provide workflows;
the routed statements provide the developer definitions.

## What does the returned evidence establish?

[RESPONSE](../../docs/RESPONSE.md) begins with an explicitly abbreviated real
envelope shape and independently captured process status. It then explains
result selection, producer ownership, admitted versus retained records, and
missing/null/unpublished/withheld distinctions. Its envelope routes reach
refusals, invocation and transport, host records, diagnostics, dossier and
fallback compilation; other command entries follow the run account.

[Select the public result](../../docs/RESPONSE.md#contract-envelope-data-select)
retains both inherited prerequisites: the host reply is present, and client
capture and host-reply admission succeeded. First-match `finished` still
selects true/0 independently of failed attempts and integrity findings. A raw
`finished` label after refusal cannot borrow that result. Admitted
`not_invoked`, unknown submission, invocation uncertainty and lost replies
also keep separate consequences. The once-only protocol is linked at the point
where uncertainty would otherwise invite an unsafe inference about resubmission.

The two exercises supply the most useful bridge to the field reference. The
example exercise identifies input rather than inventing a success transcript,
keeps query B separate from read C, and requires independent samples. The
controlled read distinguishes inode replacement, an unseen suffix and a read
returning its full bound without establishing EOF. Both preserve the limit
that sampled before/after equality does not prove continuous stability.

The difficult transition remains the boundary between a consistent record and
correspondence with an independently arranged target. The prose explains that
the aggregate projection and controller checks do not reopen the file; the
harness observer supplies a separate descriptor and samples. The developer
must still inspect the retained case's exact associations and equipment before
crediting a particular execution. This is an appropriate residual.

## Which public commitments deserve examination after a change?

[CONTRACT](../../docs/CONTRACT.md) opens with the four review outcomes and
distinguishes declaration ownership, statement placement and support. Its
commitment index gives subject homes; its changed-item index labels authored
declarations, imported record declarations, extracted examples, interface
associations and source/check support separately. Identity, shared definitions,
surface history, applicability, findings, gaps and support details remain here;
request/response field definitions follow their new homes.

The index provides concrete entry points for `run.option.runner_mode`,
`result.admitted_finished`, `EXAMPLE_DOCUMENT`, `render_template` and
`attempt_file_read_fresh`. A support row is a candidate for review, not a
claim that every edit to its file changes that promise. Evidence-detail links
return to the local support paragraph, which retains assertion scope and
limitations. Surface support inherited across documents keeps its original
scope instead of acquiring a new document-wide scope.

The difficult transition is from a file-level association to the behavior
actually affected by an edit. That judgment remains with the reviewer. In
particular, the controlled-read controller path is indexed through
`porcelain_native.rs: pub(super) fn validate`, not a fabricated direct
association for every helper. The source/check chain and RESPONSE exercise
make the trace possible without asserting a universal dependency graph.

## Whole-protocol rereading and promotion decision

Starting in ARCHITECTURE, all three required promises can be read whole without
assembling missing clauses from disconnected subjects:

- [Collection before release](../../docs/ARCHITECTURE.md#collection-before-release)
  states the phase barrier, collection failures, worker ownership, numbered
  release and acknowledgement, publication and post-reap observation. It now
  distinguishes ordinary prior completion from actor availability,
  confirmed-reap and cancellation gates. The linked scheduler obligation and
  ordering assessment retain those separate scopes. `CWorker.swift`,
  `ActorScheduler.walkSchedule`, `ActorSession`, `eligibleOrderedStep` and
  `buildOrdering` are appropriate mechanisms for this account. The newly
  corrected actor rule's support identifies failed-setup/death assertions and
  the independent process-absence observation; it credits check reading only.
- [Reservation and execution](../../docs/ARCHITECTURE.md#contract-transport-delivery)
  retains effect-free acquisition, control admission, the fresh deadline sample,
  invocation marking before the execution proxy, one execution call, preserved
  uncertainty and the original continuous-time allowance. Adjacent authorization
  and control records clarify their own boundaries. Returned capture meanings
  and inherited transport support remain linked to RESPONSE with their original
  scope; no execution replay or total controller-drain deadline is invented.
- [Custody protocol](../../docs/ARCHITECTURE.md#contract-extensions-custody)
  retains preparation before launch/application, one-shot issue publication,
  host custody, recipient framing acknowledgement, byte comparison and
  settlement, source-answer observation and erasure at last use/teardown.
  `AuthorityCustody`, the worker exchange/service functions and actor schedule
  explain the mechanism. Native call returns, custody acceptance, memory erasure
  and subsequent access remain different observations. Required setup refusal
  and unresolved actor termination keep their consequences.

No additional subject promotion is justified by these readings. The seven
named incoming subjects remain complete. The frame and validator statements
likewise retain structural versus semantic admission and independent native
versus host framing/association scopes; their links to returned records do not
substitute those records for the protocol.

## Maintenance duties added, removed and retained

Meaning remains authored once in the surface tree. New presentation duties are
to route each surface introduction and complete direct subject in
`presentation.json`, maintain declared region markers, and review tour-section
associations against what their prose actually explains. A move requires
checking inherited applicability/support, positional referents and inbound
links. CONTRACT's reading introduction is separately authored; indexes derive
from existing owners and relationships.

The old `part` placement fact and positional output assumptions no longer supply
the workflow. Maintainers no longer edit meaning merely to move a statement or
assume CONTRACT is every statement's destination. Stamp-table owners remain
separate from all generated destinations. No second schema, universal change
classifier or obligation to connect every field to help/example/test-input was
introduced.

Regeneration, current-output checks, historical derivation and integrated/native
evidence retain different scopes. Limits/build/architecture owners regenerate
their own inputs before contract generation; generated contract spans are copied
values. Public changes still require payload review; source, protocol and
boundary changes still require their appropriate identity updates and existing
checks, including the ordering mutation control when its protected mechanism
changes. The frozen guide's later rewrite and final build/evidence accounting
remain the executor's handoff responsibilities, not evidence supplied by this
editorial reading.
