# Hint activation acceptance

Associated with the worker wait exploration plan. Status: frozen CPU screen.
The ARM option for this tail closed without adoption on its selected CPU
criterion. The candidate changes only the unsuccessful activation
wait batch from 1,000 to 10,000 `yield` hints in a signed disposable source
substitution. It adds no dependency, acquired resource, state or publication.
Actual selected source and binary identities are frozen below before execution;
production and its signed app remain unchanged.

The pilot's 0.26–0.28 microsecond pass periods nominate less frequent clock and
predicate checks, rather than a faster response. Multiplying the hint batch by
ten is the sole alternative in this round; its latency cannot be inferred by
multiplying the old pass period. Source counts are not CPU or energy evidence.
Fresh extended-hold baselines on HOST-04/HOST-06 measure about one CPU-second
per second. This small change must save **1% of baseline CPU rate beyond the
endpoint/elapsed allowances in every pair**, a lower worthwhile threshold than
ARM's added platform dependency. Do not accept solely on a count ratio.

Use the unchanged cost and neutral readers from the ARM comparisons, five
alternating pairs per rapid and one-second-idle regime, one excluded first
request per exact staged executable, no additional sampling or replacement.
Require eligible gaps (rapid 0–100 ms, idle 1–1.25 s), stable power and no sleep,
actual held generation, queries, ordering, effect guards, sensitivity and owned
endings. Holds are at least one second or 100 accounting quanta and characterize
CPU demand only. Activation observes the parked unapplied issuer with an
applied held subject and extension custody; its restricted-profile behavior
would still need qualification before any adoption.

The CPU criterion uses candidate upper rate versus baseline lower rate after
one declared quantum per endpoint and full elapsed brackets; the saving must
be at least 1% of baseline upper rate. Other worker, host, validator and observer
held CPU remain visible. Whole-request cost, response, energy and peer capacity
are unmeasured in this screen. A negative result closes the candidate without
adoption, not all possible hint counts. A positive result requires the full
2 ms response gate with at most 0.5 ms combined uncertainty, wait-specific
behavior controls, order mutations, signed verification and resolved aggregate
tradeoffs before acceptance. No energy claim follows from unchanged CPU.

Raw evidence is dispatcher-owned, gitignored and local. Announce a five-minute
maximum window; the measurement watchdog is 60 seconds with 60 seconds reserved
for owned cleanup. Release has at most two busy processes; activation at most
three, including the sensitivity control. Builds are serial and capped.
