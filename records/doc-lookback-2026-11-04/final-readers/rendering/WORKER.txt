The Worker: Instrument and Constraints
A worker must keep reporting under the policy it is testing while leaving the requested operations exposed to that policy. PolicyWitness prepares its report storage
before application, then opens file targets, looks up Mach services and creates exec resources inside the attempts. Moving a target open across that boundary
changes the experiment: a later read would exercise an already held descriptor, with the authority that acquiring it supplied.
The boundary is policy application inside a running process. The executable, libraries, memory and process context are already present. A fresh worker applies its
policy once: the one worker of the single-worker form, or one worker per actor of the actor form, where an unapplied actor applies none. Its later phases share that
process and can observe effects of earlier actions, and in the actor form an actor can hold authority it issued itself. The unsandboxed host arranges queries,
collects publications and replies. This tour explains the resources and constraints of that instrument. The architecture tour owns orchestration and ordering; the 
build tour owns compilation and provenance; the evidence contract owns the meaning of its records.

What the worker can establish
The worker performs admitted actions and publishes native observations. Its inputs include attempt kinds and targets, bounded arguments, phase partitions and
time allowances; requested extension actions maintain local handle state. Prediction belongs to the validator. The host owns disposition, collection and comparison,
and the controller gathers log candidates. Those responsibilities are a deliberate division of work, not observations the worker failed to make.
The observations themselves have limits. Native return values and local publications do not establish private kernel extension state, effective permissions,
revocation or the cause of a permission-shaped failure. Nor do they show whether privacy consent mediation affected an attempt; the external-runner guide gives
target-selection guidance for that limitation. Joining host observations and log candidates to a result does not automatically justify causal attribution or a verdict that
prediction and enforcement agree. The evidence ownership table and comparison contract define those boundaries.
A native effect and a published result are also distinct. A call can create a file before the worker dies without publishing its result; the host must then withhold that
result even though an independent observer can find the file. Native observations and publications explains the publication boundary and its create-then-kill control.
Other omissions are measurement gaps: a live signal-mask observation and a complete initial Mach-right census are absent from the launch evidence, not
unknowable.
Reporting apparatus
The host's ActorSession, used by both drivers, creates an evidence object, requests its immediate unlink, sizes and maps it, initializes the request and touches its
pages. The worker's map_region maps the received descriptor before compilation. The mapping holds request data, publication payloads and atomic control fields.
The host's mapping remains available during collection even when worker cleanup is unconfirmed. The C harness checks publication under its bare-deny and full-
region cases.
The worker retains its evidence descriptor as well as the mapping. Its policy input and readiness descriptors also stay open until exit. Policy EOF ends source
delivery and the host closes its readiness reader after the wait, while the worker's references remain. Launch observation L saw those endpoints in both of its
recorded launches.
Request, result and token storage have fixed capacities from the limits inventory. The policy buffer and consumed-parameter snapshot are static worker storage.
Temporary frames hold timing arithmetic, hash state, argv pointers and diagnostics. Host-side Swift arrays and copied launch strings remain host allocations;
spawn copies argument contents into the new image. The worker checks the received source length and bounded fields, then passes the policy and parameter
snapshot to libsandbox. Its compiled-object path is main → pw_capture_profile when requested → application. PW explicitly frees the parameter object and a
returned compile-error string on the corresponding paths. The compiled profile has no explicit PW destruction call; ownership changes inside the private apply
routine remain unobserved.
The optional compiler receipt uses the already mapped evidence region and reads worker memory before apply. The host decodeProfileCapture validates and
copies the published receipt. Its relation to compiled bytes, application and effective permissions is defined by the receipt contract.

Starting authority and ambient context
A worker starts with the authority and restrictions of its credentials, signed image and inherited context. Built-in workers carry empty entitlements; external runners
supply a separately signed worker, whose read-back entitlements are checked against the specimen's required keys by enforce_required_entitlements. The 
external-runner guide and signing reference own that selection and identity account. Explicit augments change the submitted policy and are recorded as such.
Host spawn actions select the policy, evidence and readiness endpoints, plus the optional authority endpoint. The single-worker launch uses nil spawn attributes
and leaves other descriptors to native close-on-exec inheritance. Its standard output/error are inherited, and the evidence descriptor can also survive under its
original number if later file actions leave that number intact. The host passes nil envp. The examined Apple spawn wrapper forwards that pointer and the examined 
exec path constructs an empty environment. In its two recorded empty allow-default launches, L found matching plain and environment-displaying process output,
inherited null-device standard output/error, and the expected protocol endpoints. This observation is separate from the source account of inheritance. The
contaminated-worker control separately establishes a configuration in which an extra descriptor and environment value reach the worker.
The worker inherits cwd, umask, limits and signal state. It ignores SIGPIPE before compilation. Exec-child setup selects descriptor isolation and a new process
group, while leaving ignored-signal inheritance and the signal mask to native spawn rules. Its empty environment and selected standard descriptors have
independent inheritance controls. The observer used by those controls itself ignores SIGPIPE, so that observation covers environment and descriptors. For a
declaration-form extension request, preparation sets both core limits to zero after resolving the private entry points; local failure is recorded. Public admission in 
extensionMeaningFailure refuses an exec attempt combined with a declaration. Actor normalization excludes exec only in authority-participating actors, including
recipients.
Worker launch setup checks file-action initialization, endpoint duplication, evidence unlink and descriptor flags, and spawn attributes before dispatch in 
ActorSession. The host setup controls inject failures in disposable source copies and observe refusal and cleanup. Copied-argument allocation in 
withCStringArrayCopy remains unchecked; failed copies can produce empty or truncated argv. Issuer handle initialization in ExtensionSession has fixture-
contained invalid-handle use, so its no-spawn result is not proof of production handling of an invalid native handle. An injected unlink failure leaves an owned empty
shared-memory name for the fixture to remove.
The single-worker form retains its launch arrangement: the low-descriptor control can allocate evidence at fd 0, which a later policy duplication overwrites. Actor
launches relocate sources above their destinations and use close-on-exec by default, explicitly open stdout/stderr on /dev/null, and supply an empty
environment. A separate kernel descriptor inventory checks actual parked actors with a deliberately inheritable host descriptor and closed host stdin; their explicit
endpoints survive and the unrequested descriptors do not.
Launch observation L
The launch-inventory case observed two separate workers with empty allow-default plans, no extensions and no validator queries. Existing pre-ready and post-
apply sleep overrides selected the two observation windows. Source order, selected controls and elapsed time locate the observations; the observer did not read
publication flags live. Both captures showed numeric descriptors 0–4, read/write null-device stdout/stderr, cwd /, and identical plain and environment-displaying ps
output. vmmap returned an inventory; lsmp refused task-port inspection. Both workers later published completion and were reaped; the pre-ready worker reported
readiness EPIPE after the host's readiness wait expired.
These are observations of the recorded app and those two launch configurations. They do not measure a transition within one process, establish an empty Mach-
right set or identify an unnamed mapping as the reporting object. Extension-bearing workers, deny-policy acquisitions, live signal state, umask and resource limits
were outside the observation.
L's receipt identifies run 20261018T223601Z_85d8418b, executed from revision f85c7b094d75bf72931cd4856cb4ad086da20cbe against the signed app accepted
with runtime implementation a458b28. The app's own build stamp remains v0.3.1-39-g6380645-dirty; its inventory matches that accepted app. Worker source
identity is 4cb19db043606894ace2e978434f8805edf4c79245f3588cc68a33b36deca9e8. The receipt retains the command, specimens, source hashes, selected
raw captures, final worker evidence and hashes of the original artifacts. The original complete raw output and app-inventory comparison record are unavailable in
this checkout and are not pinned in tests/RETAINED.json. The copied observations and hashes describe the dated run; they do not establish current-source
applicability.
Reuse for another implementation or environment requires an applicability review or a new observation; matching source hashes establish source identity, not that
the relevant conditions were exercised.
Known gap: L covers two recorded launches, and the actor descriptor controls cover specified endpoints and injected descriptor contamination. Live signal masks,
resource limits, a complete Mach-right census and private runtime state remain unobserved; native inheritance and loader behavior remain part of the instrument's
received substrate, without a complete isolation or effective-authority census.

The existing-file control checks preserved bytes and object identity in its case. The fresh-read contract defines which native observations and bounded bytes reach
the reply.
Exec setup is also inside the attempt, after apply. exec_resources_acquire creates the output pipes and initializes opaque spawn handles, checks setup results
and calls cleanup for initialized resources on failure. The examined Apple spawn implementation allocates both handles and can grow action storage. That is a
published-source account; allocation events in the installed libraries were unobserved. PW's post-apply diagnostics also call libc formatting and error-string routines
into fixed destinations. The examined formatting implementation and error-string implementation have path-dependent internal behavior, including a retained
fallback error buffer. Runtime allocator, locale and binding behavior remain unobserved at these call sites.
The per-attempt resource controls exercise a descriptor ceiling and a bare-deny setup path. The ceiling checks reuse across attempts; the denying case observes
setup reaching spawn for its profile and environment. Source establishes post-apply placement. The child receives explicit null-device stdin, captured stdout/stderr,
an empty environment and a new process group. PW drains output and observes the leader under the execution budgets. A nonzero child exit after successful
spawn still supplies spawn evidence; the exec action contract keeps spawn, child exit and cleanup meanings separate.
attempt_exec_spawn retains leader ownership during observation and targets its process group for exceptional cleanup. The optional exec_observation records
the collection snapshot before cleanup, actual group/fallback call results and the final leader wait separately, behind slot publication. Pending or failed waits have
no status; clock faults and local deadlines do not fabricate native errno. Missing metadata establishes no cleanup fact. Worker death can interrupt cleanup; the host
supervises the worker. The lifecycle controls observe the exercised leader/descendant paths independently.
Known gap: exec descendants can outlive later steps and worker exit. Ordinary completion requests no termination, so same-group descendants that close their
streams can survive; worker death can also leave descendants alive. Exceptional cleanup requests target the owned group, but descendants that leave it escape
those requests. Signal returns, EOF and leader reap do not establish group absence.
Mach lookup acquires a bootstrap send-right reference, then a service right on successful lookup. attempt_mach_lookup attempts to deallocate the service right
and leaves the acquired bootstrap reference until process exit, including after lookup failure and repeated attempts. This lifetime is source-established; a live right-
count measurement is absent. attempt_sysctl_read uses an attempt-local stack buffer and publishes call status, discarding the returned sysctl value. The
inventory includes that buffer among local working storage. Unlink and access calls acquire no PW-owned target handle at their call sites.
Supplied extension authority
The declaration form supplies additional authority deliberately. The host resolves the target and records its metadata, then a fresh ordinary-authority issuer requests
a native file token. The issuer and host have their own channel views, separate from worker evidence. Host delivery requires bounded accepted issuance and
confirmed clean issuer reaping. ExtensionSession owns that preparation and its later host erasure.
The worker's prepare_extension resolves consume/release symbols before apply, changes the core limit on the prepared path, maps and validates the optional
channel, and attempts to close its descriptor once. Early main refusals can precede that preparation. Accepted token bytes remain a borrowed view of the shared
mapping. Requested consume and release calls occur in attempt_extension, inside an attempt after apply. The static handle carries state across actions and
phases; a native result other than the consume failure sentinel establishes the handle used by a later requested release.
Worker erasure occurs when preparation rejects a mapped channel, after the consume decision on paths reaching that cleanup, before the post-phase test seams,
and on every ordinary departure after a channel has been accepted. erase_accepted_token clears the full fixed buffer, including bytes beyond the token's
terminating NUL. Terminal paths erase before releasing done; cancellation and early returns erase without adding publications. Fatal termination or an interrupted
native call need not reach cleanup. Erasure does not call native extension release. The accepted mapping remains until exit. The host separately erases its
channel before reply preparation and again at session destruction; its observation belongs to the host. In issuer main, bounded length inspection governs copying
and erasure before free. An oversized token is rejected and that inspected prefix is erased; the allocation extent beyond the bound is unobserved.
Fresh reads obtain a new descriptor at each requested action, so before-consume, during-use and after-release reads each test fresh acquisition at that point. The
worker publishes the native release result and any subsequent requested read observations. The extension checks accept either result for the after-release read
and verify returned content when successful. The native evidence contract defines the limits of native results and local publication.
4
In the actor form, prepare_authority resolves the issue, consume and release entry points, lowers the core limit, maps the actor's authority region and validates
its identity and index before application. Required channel setup failure prevents the scheduler from releasing that actor's phases; missing native symbols instead
leave individual operations unavailable. The recipient snapshots each inbound slot's assigned source, action and delivery before application and checks them on
publication. attempt_extension_issue calls sandbox_extension_issue_file once with the exact submitted bytes, publishes nonnull or NULL with the saved
errno and copies a bounded token into that issue's assigned outbound slot. It erases the native allocation to its inspected length and frees it before publishing the
slot. Published outbound bytes belong to the host, which copies or rejects and erases them; timeout and worker exit do not overwrite bytes a host may still read. 
attempt_extension_transfer publishes into that transfer's own cell and waits for its answer while servicing its own inbound slots, so a transfer to itself cannot
wait on itself. Actor consume reads only the inbound slot this actor accepted and the host settled as usable for the named delivery; the handle table is indexed by
the plan's handle references rather than one static handle. Parked actors accept or reject deliveries in service_inbound_once without consuming. Ordinary exit
erases settled inbound buffers. The host erases remaining shared buffers only for reaped or never-launched owners; unresolved termination permits detaching its
own mapping, not clearing bytes under a potentially live worker. Private host custody has separate erasure ownership and does not depend on a worker
publication.
Local release state differs by form. Declaration execution sets release_decided before testing eligibility or making the native call. Actor execution retains its
handle-valid bit, but admission rejects a second release of that handle. Neither form provides a public release retry or an automatic exit-time native release. A
native release result is separate from later fresh-access evidence.
Known gap: private extension calls may allocate or retain internal state that PW cannot inspect. Erasure observations cover PW-owned transient buffers;
successful native results and phase progress do not establish kernel extension state, stable identity, effective permissions or access revocation.
Native observations and publications
A native call can complete before the worker publishes the attempt slot. pw_progress records the latest atomic milestone, while run_attempt releases the
completed slot after writing its payload. The host reads completed payload after observing publication. The create-then-kill control independently observes the
created file while requiring the reply to withhold the unpublished result. Partially written payload remains outside the evidence the host is entitled to decode. Native
call-boundary flags and result publication have the meanings in the worker evidence contract.
The worker's release wait uses atomic fields, continuous-clock samples and local arithmetic/text. Refusal publishes done and exits; cancellation exits without
adding done or acknowledgement. After the phase loop and any selected post-phase seam, publish_done_and_exit releases done and calls _exit(0) without
waiting for the host.
In actor mode the worker also parks before each visit in an activation wait built from the same atomic fields, continuous-clock samples and relax batch as the
release wait. It accepts only the global ordinal its next phase record names and publishes that ordinal back; a greater ordinal is a published refusal, an expired
shared lifetime cutoff is a published timing failure, and cancellation exits without done. A parked actor services inbound transport without running requested native
actions; the apparatus cost is one runnable polling loop per parked actor. A cutoff already past at clock setup refuses before any policy byte. An unapplied actor
publishes its declared setup after the ready byte and never sets applied, and every actor publishes its own hash of the policy bytes it read beside the host's hash
of the submitted source. Single-worker requests never enter the activation wait. Public actor requests use this mode, exercised by harness activation controls, actor
driver tests and the public request contract. The lifetime cutoff is sampled in checked waits; it does not preempt a blocked native call or bound final cleanup. The
host retains its evidence mapping after worker exit and independently observes reaping. Its exit request still cancels a worker waiting for release; grace and
termination remain fallback cleanup. These paths use existing storage and automatic frames. Explicit PW dynamic acquisitions after apply are concentrated in the
attempted operations; the source-level inventory ends at native/library call boundaries whose internals remain unobserved.
Earlier actions and external actors can change conditions before a later attempt, even with an intact release hold. Unqualified raw query records remain available
after a refusal; they do not establish whether each native call encountered a live target. The ordering account and ordering evidence states define eligibility for 
query_first and what an acknowledged release establishes after abnormal exit.
