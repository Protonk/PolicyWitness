## X02 — Original request and carried-record operands

**Public nodes:** `diagnostics.execution.project`, `.actor_rows`, `.families`, `.reply_checks`; `actor_evidence.actor.reader_scope`; `actor_evidence.gate.check`, `.prepared_association`, `.evaluate`; `profile.receipt.request_nonce`, `.labels`. **Current promise and inspected realization:** the controller checks relations within the carried reply; it does not generally re-admit the original specimen or independently compare its actor/action declarations to that specimen. `porcelain_admission.rs:1576` accepts only `Option<&PWRunnerRunResult>`. Actor projection builds its action list from reply phases whose carried actor ID matches the carried actor row, then resolves their IDs against carried steps/events. `actor_associations` at line 1506 checks carried index/ID, launch/subprocess PID, visits, phase/event membership and transfer source. `porcelain_actor.rs:1713` dispatches identity/setup/equipment/application/gates/custody checks on that same reply. No original request enters these function signatures.

This is not evidence that all such relations are unchecked. It establishes exactly where their operands come from. Lifecycle/action count and identity checks (`porcelain_admission.rs:516–559`) compare the lifecycle to the action list constructed from the returned account. The gate's producer association with the prepared request is a distinct host duty. A coordinated but self-consistent replacement of reply actor/action declarations with another internally coherent account is outside what this receiver alone establishes; this is an information-limit argument, not an observed substitution or native bypass.

The profile text already states the limit unusually well: `.request_nonce` says a consumer needs a separately retained request nonce; `.labels` says nonce, bytes, hashes, lengths and counts remain host assertions. `run_flow.rs:96` applies identity and `admission_problem`; it does not supply the original request nonce. I found no missing operand for the *stated controller label check*. Moving the host's capture duties onto that check would invent a stronger promise.

**Proposed retain/clarify wording:** “Controller relationship checks use the admitted reply's carried declarations and observations. Passing them establishes the checked relationships within that account; it does not independently compare the account with the original request. Producer checks against the prepared request and any separately retained request-to-reply comparison retain their own owners.” Place beside `diagnostics.execution.families` and cross-reference the nonce field, without weakening the individual host production rules.

**Intent needed:** none to explain existing operands. Requiring independent original-request association, or treating controller acceptance as proof of it, requires an explicit maintainer decision plus retained request operands, applicability/failure rules and independent controls. B and C agree with A's separation. A concrete existing controller rule requiring an original declaration/nonce comparison, or an uninspected caller supplying it to an additional receiver, would change this conclusion. The signatures alone do not prove what every host producer checked.

## X04 — Retention is not unconditional actor attribution

**Public nodes:** `diagnostics.execution.actor_rows`, `.reply_checks`, `.scope_example`; `diagnostics.actor.projection`; `diagnostics.issue.subject.withholding_scope` in `41-diagnostics.surface.md`.

Current behavior computes worker-local projections first. A local lifecycle failure withholds that worker projection. A later reply-wide failure withholds the aggregate, sets unknown cause and invalid integrity, and preserves the actor rows and an already computed ordinary stop reason. `porcelain_admission.rs:1342–1433,1576–1655` realizes this directly. The reply itself is borrowed, not rewritten.

The word “independent” needs precision. An actor's **projection calculation** is separate from reply-wide checking, but its operands depend on carried labels and joins. Its action list uses actor-ID/phase/action association before reply-wide validation; its output label is copied from the row. `validate_lifecycle` checks count/order/identity against that list. A rejected launch/subprocess PID association or duplicate actor identity therefore prevents treating retained `clean_exit` as independent proof that the requested actor exited cleanly. Conversely, an invalid transfer source elsewhere need not destroy the retained subprocess's observed reap/exit information. These are different dependencies.

The inspected `typed_invalid_actor_account_keeps_independent_actor_endings` at line 2434 checks actor-evidence findings alongside retained clean-exit projections and copied labels. It does **not** enumerate every association failure or prove attribution remains sound after every such failure. C06's proposed PID/action/owned-reap variations would discriminate those dependency classes; they are not evidence already obtained from this test.

**Recommended decision: retain row projections, clarify their authority. Proposed replacement for the withholding gap:** “Reply-wide findings withhold the aggregate projection and retain separately computed actor-row projections. A retained actor-row projection describes the lifecycle account in that row. It does not override a finding against the row's identity, launch association or action membership, and does not independently establish attribution to the requested actor. Consumers requiring that attribution must also require the relevant associations. Worker-local lifecycle findings continue to withhold that worker's projection.” Also replace casual “independent actor endings” prose with “separately computed actor-row projections” where it could imply authenticated identity.

This is an acceptance of current scope, not a new automatic dependency engine. Maintainer approval is needed to close the explicit gap. If the desired product instead promises conclusions safe to use without checking association findings, selective withholding must be specified and implemented; blanket withholding for every unrelated fault is not compelled by the present contract. A local projection path that re-establishes identity independently of those carried joins would change the qualification. A different fault that actually causes local validation failure is counterevidence to an overbroad claim of universal retention, not to the documented distinction.
