4. Actor form: one dossier per actor in `data.specimen.actors[]` (`run_flow.rs:634-640`).
5. When augmentation fails, imports are `not_applicable` with `failure: "augmentation_failed"` (`run_flow.rs:274-276`).
The hashes are taken from the in-memory JSON value before the `held` string is serialized at `run_flow.rs:696`; that they describe the same text the host receives is by construction, and is checked on the signed product through the reply's `policy_sha256`.
