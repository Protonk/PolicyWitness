## Maintainer packet: retain current behavior with explicit limits

Recommended package: accept the five current policies below and replace their
open behavior-gap prose with the stated definitions. Runtime bytes and owners
stay unchanged. These are decisions about public meaning even though the
implementation remains the same. The requirement for this explicit decision
is [plan section 5](../../controller/NEW-DOC-INTEGRATION-PLAN.md#5-cross-examination-and-decision-authority).

### M01 — completion result

Affected node: `envelope.data.select.success_findings`; related rules
`envelope.data.select.finished`, `.other`, `diagnostics.execution.outer_result`.

Proposed wording:

> For an admitted reply, result.ok and process exit describe the host completion
> outcome. They do not certify successful attempts or successful controller
> relationship checks. A finished reply with integrity findings remains true/0;
> callers inspect the observations and findings for those separate conclusions.

Alternative: select false/1 for explicitly enumerated failed applicable
integrity checks even when the admitted host outcome is finished. This changes
script behavior, controller mapping, consumer rules and fixtures; nonempty
issues alone cannot define invalidity because supported conflicts may carry
issues. An unsuccessful native attempt alone would remain distinct.

### M02 — actor-row projections

Affected node: `diagnostics.issue.subject.withholding_scope`; related
`diagnostics.execution.actor_rows`, `.reply_checks`, `.families`, `.scope_example`
and `diagnostics.actor.projection`.

Proposed wording:

> Reply-wide findings withhold the aggregate and retain separately computed
> actor-row projections. Each projection describes the carried subprocess and
> action records selected through carried labels and IDs. It does not override
> findings against identity, launch association or action membership, or
> independently establish attribution to the requested actor. Worker-local
> lifecycle findings still withhold that worker's projection.

Alternative: withhold affected actor projections when failed identity, PID or
action associations undermine their attribution. This needs a dependency
policy and coordinated controller, contract and consumer controls. Blanket
withholding would also discard unrelated sound local observations. All choices
retain raw records. The retained policy requires callers needing attribution
to check the relevant association findings as well as the local projection.

### M03 — degraded reporting

Affected node: `reply.failure.publish.associations`; related
`actor_evidence.actor.reader_scope` and `diagnostics.execution.families`.

Proposed wording:

> Reporting-failure admission checks the degraded form and applicable receiver
> context. It does not certify ordinary actor/phase associations in retained
> records. Those records may preserve the inconsistency that caused reporting
> failure. Worker-local lifecycle checks continue at their stated scope; no
> comparison or query-order conclusion is recovered from retained raw records.

Alternative: require ordinary associations, or a specified subset, in degraded
reports. Requiring all can force useful rich reports into minimal evidence
loss. A narrower projection policy depends on M02. Neither is a harmless
validation tightening.

### M04 — merged unconfirmed vocabulary

Affected node: `diagnostics.execution.vocabulary.vocabulary_choice`.

Proposed wording:

> The controller uses unconfirmed for both unresolved and inapplicable final
> status. The retained host claim state and reason preserve that distinction
> for consumers that need it.

Alternative: add distinct controller vocabulary, changing public values and
reader expectations. No loss of the raw distinction was found.

### M05 — separately authored classifiers

Affected node: `diagnostics.execution.reader_evidence.classifier_generation`.

Proposed wording:

> Host, controller and test readers retain separately authored semantic
> classifiers. Agreement is checked against stated definitions and independent
> expectations within each reader's scope. No generated shared classifier or
> universal equivalence is promised.

Alternative: design a shared generation scheme and an independently expected
oracle. Shared implementation alone would reduce independent semantic checks;
the investigation found no concrete mismatch requiring that work.

runtime behavior, with five open choices becoming explicit definitions. All
other support/evidence gaps retain their limits. Public snapshot change is

mere desire for a larger evidence count.

## Authoring realization

The existing tree language permits definitions beneath subjects/definitions,
not beneath reporting rules. The first generation attempt refused that illegal
nesting before writing outputs. M01 becomes `envelope.data.success_findings`,
beside and explicitly referencing `envelope.data.select`; M03 becomes
`reply.failure.associations`, beside and referencing `reply.failure.publish`
and recipient scope. Their previous gap IDs map to these definitions. The other
three IDs stay unchanged. No rule, effect, applicability or owner is moved;
the old gaps' open questions become accepted descriptive meanings. This is a
structural authoring placement within the approved wording, not a change to
the authoring language or a withdrawn commitment.

A's normalized comparison confirms unchanged existing rule/field contexts and
effects. The moved descriptive definitions inherit their new subjects' context:
M01 no longer inherits the reporting rule's `when`/`free.when`, and its `where`
is payload assembly rather than completion; M03 inherits reply assembly rather
than response encoding. Their explicit admitted/reporting-failure prose and
`uses` references retain the intended scope. This is not a claim that every
node's inherited context is byte-identical; executable rule applicability and
responsible actors are unchanged.
